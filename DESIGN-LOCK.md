# Locked design

Parts of the interface the owner has settled. **They are not to be changed** (look, structure,
order, wording, icons, spacing, behaviour) by any session unless the owner asks for it in
that session, in so many words. A flow review comes next (see `TODO.md`, "Next: flow review"); it
works around these parts and does not reopen them.

If a task seems to need a change here, stop and ask the owner first, say what would change and
why, and wait. When the owner unlocks something, change it and update this file in the same PR.

**Two fingers on the lyrics change their size (open: bigger, close: smaller; `web/src/pinch.ts`, wired in `createReader`, so it holds wherever a song is read: song page, singing, shared list; the owner asked on 2026-11-14).** It moves the same steps as A− / A+ and keeps the same saved size.

**The key in a bar (the owner asked on 2026-11-23):** from 900px the song page's bar carries "Solo hoy" (while there is a change), Guardar with its arrow, then − / + against the next button, so what appears and hides never moves the rest; the menu of Guardar and the line "Original · Mío" hang under the bar. Under 900px it is as it was (the bar's own row on the song page). **Singing is different (the owner approved the mockup on 2026-11-23): its bar carries only the − / + of the selected song (from 900px), and "Solo hoy" lives in the dock, one row of about 52px at the foot of the screen, on every screen size, only while the key is not the saved one: the key of today in amber, "hoy", "Deshacer" and Guardar with its arrow (its menu opens above the dock); a divided page adds the name of the selected song's section.** **"Mi tono" reaches the lists:** saving it moves the song in every list that follows it; precedence is only today › this list › "mi tono" › the song's own key.

**A divided page (the owner asked on 2026-11-23, after the mockups "Pantalla dividida"):** while singing, the person divides the page of a song with the next one from the "⋯" sheet (between "Letra" and "Editar", "Dividir con el canto siguiente" / "Separar los dos cantos", off with its reason when it cannot be: the last song, or the next one already divided with another), and the two songs share one page, each in a square with a one-line band (section small, title, key). The union belongs to the list (`SetlistItem.withNext`, synced, and carried by the shared link: whoever opens it sees the same pages); only the layout is the device's (side by side on a screen wider than tall, one over the other otherwise). The bar, the key tools and the dock speak of the selected square (touch one; the first at the start); the page is what a swipe, the arrows and the position ("2–3/4") move. A divided page keeps the bar open.

**Singing folds its bar to the strip 2px before the first line of the lyrics would slide under it (`barFoldPoint` in `web/src/fold.ts`; the page of a shared list folds its bar the same way), so a section's name tag is never half hidden behind the bar (the owner asked on 2026-11-15).**

**Printing is in every menu that holds a song or a list (the owner asked on 2026-11-15):** "Imprimir con acordes" and "Imprimir solo la letra", after "Editar" and before the red row, in the "⋯" of a row of Cantos (the song's own key, the one the row shows), the "⋯" of a row of Listados (only when the list has songs; every song in the key it is seen in), the "⋯" of a song inside a list (that song, in the key the list shows for it, today's included) and the sheet behind "⋯" while singing (the key on screen); they were already in the song page's sheet and the list's. The PDF is called as the song or as the list (`web/src/print/jobs.ts`).

**Every sheet and menu behaves the same (the owner asked on 2026-11-26):** on a phone a menu is a half sheet from the bottom and **every one has its handle on top, which can be pulled down** (it follows the finger and closes on release if it was heading down, a flick is enough, or comes back; made once in `createSheet`, so no sheet can lack it). From 900px a menu is floating, beside the button that opened it, with no handle (`anchored`): the "⋯" menus, "＋ Nuevo", the songs of the list, the tools while singing, "Agregar a un listado" and "Compartir" (opened from a row of another menu, they appear where that menu was). The player's grabber moves its window, and the chord sheet's handle moves between its levels. What is not a menu (the confirmation of tagging, the preview of a song) stays a sheet from the bottom on every screen. **Pull down to sync works on every screen of the app on a phone, singing included. The player is the same window dragged by its bar wherever it appears (no docked one). "Escuchar el original" is a ♪ where there is room (600px up) and only an option of the "⋯" menu under it, never both (2026-11-26).** `tests/e2e/sheets.ts` covers it.
